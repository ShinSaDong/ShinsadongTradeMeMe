## Background: US chips held at −0.65% after an 8% intraday drop

- On Nov 25 (local time), the **Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell nearly 8% early but closed down just 0.65%.** Nvidia recovered from −9% intraday to −3.91%, Micron from −13% to −4.53%, and the SK hynix ADR finished −0.75%.
- Three headwinds hit: the USTR proposed **"overproduction" tariffs** on 14 economies, **core PCE rose 3.3% year on year, above the 3.2% forecast**, and S&P said it is **reviewing Nvidia's credit rating** over governance issues around Jensen Huang's departure and return.
- Bargain hunting ahead of Thanksgiving still cut the losses sharply. In Seoul, Samsung opened Nextrade's 8 a.m. pre-market up 1.2% and SK hynix up 2.04%.

## Today's session: the turn came after the rate hike

- **Morning:** KOSPI opened +1.01% and climbed to 4,817.77 (+2.56%); foreigners were net buyers of more than ₩230B at one point. SK hynix reached ₩1,098,000 (+6.6%).
- **Around 10 a.m., the BOK hiked:** the Monetary Policy Board raised the base rate **from 3.00% to 3.25%** — its first move in three months after back-to-back hikes in July and August and a hold in October. In the six-month rate outlook (dot plot), **eight dots sat at 3.50% and seven at 3.75%**, signaling one or two more hikes.
- **Afternoon:** foreign buying faded into net selling (−₩113.7B at the close), and the index gave back most of its gains. Of yesterday's 308.52-point drop, only 13.70 points — **less than 5%** — came back.

## Interpretation

**1. A bounce without a buyer.** Only institutions (mainly brokerages) bought, ₩750B. Foreigners bought in the morning and sold in the afternoon. Not nearly enough to undo a 6% one-day drop.

**2. Retail sold what it bought yesterday.** Individuals bought ₩1.8T yesterday and sold ₩1.0T today — quickly unloading dip purchases into the bounce. That makes it hard to count on retail buying to hold a bottom.

**3. The rate hike doused the chip rebound.** The BOK raised its growth forecasts to 3.9% for this year and 4.1% for next on the chip boom — and raised rates. Chips boom, rates rise, chip stocks get pressured: an ironic loop. SK hynix going from +6.6% intraday to +2.23% at the close is the result.

**4. Money scattered into themes.** SpaceX's approval for space data-center satellites sent aerospace and energy names flying (OCI Holdings +13%), and Samsung SDI rose 6%. Korean Air fell 6% on surging oil. There was no large-cap leadership to lift the index.

## What to watch tomorrow

- **US Thanksgiving holiday.** With Wall Street closed tonight, Korea opens tomorrow without a US cue. On days like this, **Binance's Korean-stock perpetuals** and Nextrade's pre-market become effectively the only directional signal.
- **Whether foreign selling continues.** Again today: buy in the morning, sell in the afternoon. We need a day when foreigners stay net buyers through the close before calling a bottom.
- **Digesting the rate hike.** With the BOK leaving room for more, banks and insurers benefit while growth stocks and chips may stay under pressure.
- **Next week:** Dec 1 Jensen Huang before House committees, Dec 4 US November jobs report, Dec 9 FOMC (a hike is favored).

## Today's conclusion

The rebound from yesterday's 6% plunge topped out at 2.5% early, and after the BOK hike foreigners sold again, leaving KOSPI up just 0.29%. More a pause in the decline than a rebound. Until foreigners buy through a full session, my position stays **[Watching]**.
