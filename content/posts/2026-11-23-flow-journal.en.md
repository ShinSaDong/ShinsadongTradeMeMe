## Interpretation

**1. Retail bought aggressively at the lows.** As the decline passed 5% around 11 a.m., retail bought nearly ₩250B in 50 minutes. Retail piling in during panic is a classic pattern, but they sold again into the rebound and still finished as net sellers.

**2. Foreigners were trading, not taking a direction.** They bought over ₩180B early, flipped to selling within an hour, and sold down to around −₩200B around the sidecar. I read the morning buying as a short trade on an oversold move, not a trend change.

**3. Institutions: holding → selling together → buying back after the sidecar.** In the morning institutions absorbed up to about ₩270B, but once the index fell below −5% after 11 a.m., they sold too (about ₩210B). Only after the sidecar did they start buying again, producing the rebound after 12:30. **Institutions made today's rebound**; foreigners sold to the end.

**4. Hopes for US semiconductors also fed the post-sidecar rebound.** Major chip stocks were rebounding 3–5% on the Nasdaq side during our session, and I think that contributed to the narrowing loss after the sidecar. But without large foreign buying in Korea, the rebound was weak, and indeed it slipped again just before the close. News that the government is preparing to deploy the stock market stabilization fund also came out, but it did not stop today's decline.

## What to watch tomorrow

- **Whether foreigners turn to net buying in cash stocks.** The key is whether it lasts into the afternoon, not just a brief morning burst.
- **Whether semiconductors actually rebound in the US regular session.** Watch Nvidia and other AI/chip large caps and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index.
- **Whether the VIX falls below 30.** If it stays around 35, this week remains volatile.
- **When and how much of the stabilization fund is deployed.**

## Today's conclusion

On a day with a sidecar for the second straight session, retail bought the lows, institutions held, sold along, then bought back, and foreigners sold to the end. **Unless foreigners return with large buying, KOSPI isn't there yet.** This week I won't rush to catch a crash — I'm **[Watching]**.
