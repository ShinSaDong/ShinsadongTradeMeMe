KOSPI closed above 5,000 for the first time in three sessions since the 19th, and **foreigners turned net buyers for the first time in 8 sessions**. In the regular session Samsung Electronics rose 6.70%, SK hynix 6.73% and Hyundai Motor 11.11%.

## Background: Jensen Huang returns → Nasdaq's biggest gain in 1 year 7 months

- On the 23rd (US time), Nvidia's board **unanimously decided to bring Jensen Huang back as CEO**, 11 days after he stepped down.
- **Nvidia +31.5%**, **Nasdaq +6.51% (24,089.56)**, S&P 500 +4.73%, Dow +3.01%. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index jumped about 14%.
- Korea opened up 3%+, crossed 5,000 within two minutes, and at 09:06 **buy-side sidecars** were triggered on both KOSPI and KOSDAQ.
- But the same day the US Commerce Department **added Megaspeed, the Singapore company at the center of the Huang scandal, to its export-control list.** Whether Nvidia's advanced chips reached China through it is the core allegation, so a spark remained.

## Regular session: inverse positions flushed out, a clear rebound

- I think the size of the rebound came from **inverse and short positions built up during last week's and yesterday's sell-side sidecars being unwound all at once**. Inverse ETFs were weak today, and money flowed into leveraged ETFs.
- By sector, chips rose 6–7%, roughly with the index, while **large-cap substrates (Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek) and robots were stronger.** Humanoid-robot ETFs gained about 17%.
- The **small/mid-cap PCB names** that jumped during yesterday's decline (Isu Petasys, Korea Circuit, Daeduck Electronics) were weaker today.

## Bad news after the close: after-hours giveback

After 4 p.m., in the after-hours session (KRX and Nextrade, until 20:00), the mood changed. As US stock futures fell across the board and **Nvidia dropped about 4% in pre-market trading**, chips and substrates that had rallied in the regular session gave back around 4% each. In the US, talk of a December Fed rate hike spread, adding caution ahead of the PCE inflation release on the 25th.

## Overnight in the US: Nasdaq chips slump

- On the 24th (US time), the Dow rose but the **Nasdaq 100 fell 1.99% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index plunged 7.88%**, reversing in a single day.
- **Nvidia −5.86%, AMD −8.86%, Micron −11.01%, SK hynix ADR −9.90%.** Profit-taking after the 31% surge combined with renewed governance concerns and debate over AI overinvestment.
- With SK hynix's ADR down nearly 10%, Korean chips are likely to start today (25th) under pressure.

## Interpretation

**1. The rebound was real, but it was a short squeeze.** Inverse positions were flushed out and foreigners and institutions bought together, so the index clearly rose. But the force came more from unwinding bets on a decline than from fresh buying, which is why it gave back so easily once the session ended.

**2. Foreigners opened it; institutions grew it.** Foreigners passed ₩700B by 11:30 a.m. and barely added more; institutions drove the afternoon (−₩164B in the morning → +₩869B at the close). Retail sold ₩2T into the rebound.

**3. After-hours and the US session turned it back down.** Chips and substrates gave back most of their gains after hours, and overnight the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 7.88% and SK hynix's ADR 9.90%. A one-day surge was not a trend change.

**4. Robots held to the end.** Robots are the only sector back above the 19th. Chips and substrates are still swinging with Nvidia and US semiconductors.

## What to watch through next week

- **The 25th PCE inflation data and the Fed's December path.** Growing hike expectations would keep pressure on tech.
- **Whether Nvidia's governance and China export-control issues** (after the Megaspeed sanctions) spread further.
- **Whether foreign net buying was a one-day event.** Whether they turn back to selling on the 25th is the first check.
- **Whether KOSPI holds 5,000.** A slide back to the 4,700s (the 23rd's close) would mean this rebound ended as a short squeeze.

## Today's conclusion

The regular session rebounded decisively as inverse positions were flushed out, and foreigners returned after 8 sessions. But chips and substrates were pushed back after hours, and Nasdaq semiconductors plunged again overnight. Rather than chasing the rebound, it's time to see whether the bad news is digested — **[Watching] through the first week of December.**
