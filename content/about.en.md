**ShinsadongTradeMeMe** is a blog that helps with trading Korean stocks. It puts the **long/short ratios** of Korean stock perpetual futures listed on Binance (Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, Hyundai Motor, LG Electronics, NAVER, Samsung Electro-Mechanics and more) side by side with the Korean market's **buy/sell ratios and retail, foreign and institutional flows**.

## What you can find here

- **Market Flow**: Collects retail, foreign, institutional and program-trading flows for KOSPI, KOSDAQ and KOSPI200 futures every minute, and analyzes a market mood score (−100 to +100) and which flows drove the index.
- **Dashboard**: Compares long/short account ratios, top-trader positions and taker-buy ratios for the 8 Korean stock futures on Binance with Korean intraday buy/sell ratios and investor flows.
- **Scalping**: Finds 6 practical patterns on 1/3/5-minute bars of the top 50 stocks by volume, and calculates entry, stop and target prices and timing.
- **Blog**: Explains how to read the indicators and interpret trading setups, plus daily flow journals.

## About the author

An embedded software developer and individual investor in Korean stocks. I record the tools I built because I needed them for my own trading, and the notes I make while watching the market.

## Notes

All data and articles are for reference only and are not investment advice. Data may be delayed or inaccurate. Binance's Korean stock futures cannot be traded by Korean residents; this site only shows public market data as reference indicators.

*Most posts were originally written in Korean and translated into English. Amounts are often shown in Korean units: 1억 (eok) = ₩100 million, 1조 (jo) = ₩1 trillion.*
