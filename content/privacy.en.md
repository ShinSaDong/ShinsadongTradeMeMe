ShinsadongTradeMeMe (the "Site") values your privacy and handles information as follows.

## 1. Information collected

The Site has no sign-up and does not directly collect personal information such as names or contact details. However, connection IP addresses, browser information and visit records may be logged automatically by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) while operating the service.

## 2. Cookies and advertising

The Site may display Google AdSense ads. Third-party ad vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

- See [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) for details on how Google uses advertising cookies.
- You can opt out of personalized ads in [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- You can block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may be limited.

## 3. Visit statistics

The Site uses **Google Analytics** for visit statistics. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized usage information such as pages viewed, time on page, referral source, and device and approximate region, and does not collect information that identifies individuals. Collected data is processed under Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you do not want this, install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or block cookies in your browser. Visit counts may also be measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

## 3-1. External data requests

To display market data, the Dashboard sends requests directly from your browser to Binance's public API (fapi.binance.com). Your IP address may be shared with that service in the process.

## 4. Alerts

- When you turn on **push alerts**, the "delivery address" your browser creates (push subscription) and the alert conditions you choose are stored on the Site's server (Cloudflare), along with your display language. We do not receive names, phone numbers or email addresses. Alerts are delivered through the push servers of Google, Apple, Microsoft and Mozilla.
- Turning push off with the 🔔 button deletes the subscription immediately, and subscriptions that no longer work because you revoked notification permission in your browser are deleted automatically.
- **PC tab alert** settings and alert history are stored only in your browser (localStorage).

## 5. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the email on the [Contact](/en/contact/) page.
