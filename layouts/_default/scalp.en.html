Pattern rules summary

The rules used in the analysis. See the scalping pattern guide for details.
PatternDetectionEntryStop
① Fake breakdownBreaks the prior 20-bar low (support), then recovers above it within 3 bars on ≥1.2× average volumeSupport reclaim priceBreakdown low − 1 tick
② True support3+ lows within ±0.3% over the last 40 bars, bounce volume > decline volumeTop of support zoneBottom of support − 2 ticks
③ Long upper wickUpper wick ≥ 2× body and 50% of bar range, bar 1.5× longer than average, after a spike or at the day highDon't chase · warning stays until the high is reclaimed
④ Opening range breakoutBreaks the first-15-minute high on ≥1.3× average volume; bonus on a successful retestRange high + 1 tickHigh − 30% of range
⑤ MA pullbackMA5 > MA20 with MA20 rising; pulls back to the MAs on falling volume, then an up bar on rising volumeClose of the bounce barPullback low / MA20 − 1 tick
⑥ Prior-high breakout & retestBreaks the intraday high on 1.5× average volume → holds the old high on the pullback → rises againRe-break close / prior highPrior high −0.5% / pullback low
Targets: T1 = 1.5× the risk (or a resistance between 1.5× and 3×), T2 = 2.5× the risk. Penalty when the stop is wider than 2%, larger penalty above 3%. Score adjustments: above VWAP +8 / below −12, 5-min trend up +10 / down −15, long upper wick −20, time-of-day penalties (right after the open, near the close, after-market).
