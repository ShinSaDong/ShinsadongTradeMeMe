    How to read it

    SituationInterpretation
    Foreigners buy both cash and futuresThe strongest bullish combination. Selling both is the weakest.
    Foreigners sell cash + buy futuresMay be a hedge of cash selling or arbitrage, so the directional signal is weak.
    Heavy non-arbitrage program sellingForeigners/institutions selling large caps as a basket. Directly pushes the index down.
    Only retail net buyingRetail absorbing what foreigners and institutions sell. Rebounds in down markets are often weak.
    Index up + more declinersConcentrated in a few large caps. Don't judge individual stocks by the index alone.
    Flow with high leading correlationThat investor group is moving ahead of the index today. Watch for a turn in that flow first.
